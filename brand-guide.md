# Something Studio — Brand guide (working concept)

## Brand foundation

**Working name:** XXX studio 
**Descriptor:** Creative Technology Studio / Estudio creativo de tecnología  
**Positioning:** A bilingual creative technology studio that combines strategy, design, and engineering to make useful digital experiences and tools for businesses.  
**Audience:** Small and growing businesses, founders, independent professionals, and organizations across Puerto Rico.  
**Brand promise:** Thoughtful creative work and practical technology, shaped around the real needs of people and businesses.  
**Spanish tagline:** Ideas claras. Experiencias que funcionan.  
**English tagline:** Clear ideas. Experiences that work.

## Brand personality

- Creative, but not frivolous.
- Thoughtful, clear, and approachable.
- Modern, but not cold or overly technical.
- Confident, without overpromising.
- Bilingual and welcoming to businesses throughout Puerto Rico.

## Voice and messaging

**Do:**
- Use clear, warm, direct language.
- Explain what a service helps a client do.
- Ask about the business problem before recommending a solution.
- Be honest about scope, timing, experience, and limitations.
- Use natural Spanish and plain English; avoid literal, awkward translations.

**Avoid:**
- Guaranteed sales, rankings, leads, or business outcomes.
- Buzzword-heavy claims such as “revolutionary”, “world-class”, or “AI-powered” unless they are accurate and relevant.
- Pretending concept projects are client work.

### Typography

- Headlines: Georgia or another expressive editorial serif.
- Body/UI: system sans-serif stack (Inter when available, then platform sans-serif).
- Use generous spacing, concise paragraphs, and strong hierarchy.

### Graphic language

- Soft organic forms paired with structured grids and interface-like frames.
- Rounded corners, fine borders, restrained shadows.
- Warm neutrals with sage and coral accents.
- Editorial headlines balanced with practical, readable body text.

## Service architecture

1. **Brand identity & design** — identity systems, visual direction, and design assets.
2. **Web & digital experiences** — responsive websites and digital interfaces.
3. **Custom software** — scoped tools and applications for clear business needs.
4. **Automation & integration** — connect tools and reduce repetitive manual work.

Only sell services and deliverables that can genuinely be scoped and delivered. For unfamiliar or high-complexity work, refer it to a qualified specialist or decline it.

## Pre-launch checklist

- Complete official trade-name and trademark clearance in Puerto Rico and the United States.
- Check domain and social handle availability before using the name publicly.
- Replace concept name everywhere if it changes.
- Review all service claims and pricing against actual skills and delivery capacity.
- Enable Netlify Forms and configure email notifications, or replace with another tested contact method.
- Test the website on desktop and mobile; confirm the bilingual toggle and form workflow.
- Keep concept portfolio pieces clearly labelled until actual client work is completed and permission is obtained.
